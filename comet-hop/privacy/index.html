Comet Hop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Comet Hop on Android or iOS.

Who is responsible

Csaba Fazekas (publishing as onetyonetyone), Slovakia.
Contact: onetyonetyone.1@gmail.com

Information we collect ourselves

None. We do not run any servers for the game and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal information. Your progress (scores, gems, skins and settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Comet Hop is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA (on iOS only if you allow tracking)
  • your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads
  • crash and performance diagnostics

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); without it, Google shows non-personalized ads based on our legitimate interest in funding the game (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS you can also allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchase

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details. The store only tells the game whether you own the purchase.

Children

Comet Hop is not directed at children. It is intended for players aged 13 and over, or older where the law of your country requires it (16 in Slovakia). We do not knowingly process personal data of children below these ages.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold any personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, for example through your Google account's privacy settings. You can also contact us at the email address above.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Slovakia this is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky).

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the game gets new features. The date at the top shows the latest version.
